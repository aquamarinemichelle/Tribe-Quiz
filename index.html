How to Play

  1. Choose a South African culture from the home screen
  2. Answer 10 questions about that culture
  3. Pick the correct answer from 4 options
  4. Each correct answer earns you 10 points
  5. See your results and beat your high score!

About Tribe Quiz

Tribe Quiz celebrates the rich cultural heritage of Africa. Test your knowledge of Zulu, Xhosa, Sesotho, Sepedi, Tswana, Swati, and other African traditions, language, food, and customs.

Whether you're learning about your own culture or discovering someone else's, every question is a step toward celebrating the diversity that makes Africa special. Ubuntu — "I am because we are."
